When you're about to put Next.js in Docker and deploy to a serverless container
platform like Cloud Run, `output: "standalone"` in `next.config` is almost
mandatory.

## What it does

At `next build` time, Next uses dependency tracing to copy only the files and
packages actually used at runtime into `.next/standalone/`, and emits a slim
`server.js`. So you can boot without shipping the entire `node_modules`.

## Why it helps

- **Smaller image**: from ~1GB down to ~150MB
- **Faster cold starts**: especially noticeable on scale-to-zero platforms
- **Cleaner Dockerfile**: the runtime layer only needs to copy standalone + static + public

> Heads up: `.next/static` and `public` aren't bundled into standalone
> automatically — remember to copy them separately in your Dockerfile.
